Solutions for education

Empower your whole school

Email-first intake and configurable approvals reduce manual coordination before the document is sent. School staff start with what they already have, an email or request, and Docusign and Microsoft orchestrate the rest: approvals, a data-informed consent form, signature, verified updates, storage, and follow-up intelligence.
Verified address and consent fields are written back to the linked Dynamics 365 record automatically. The signed PDF archives to a configured SharePoint location. No manual re-entry, no version confusion.
Copilot Studio can surface agreement status, insights, and next actions when the required tools and connectors are configured. Staff ask natural-language questions about consent completion, missing signatures, or upcoming deadlines.
The same control plane supports other K-12 agreement journeys with a different request type, template, data mapping, and archive rule: enrolment, staff onboarding, supplier agreements, and more.

End-to-end flow

One request. Eight controlled transitions.

Use this sequence as the talk track for a live demo or as the implementation checklist for a pilot.

01

Capture the request

Teacher submits excursion details via email or request form.

02

Classify and approve

Agreement Desk maps to excursion process and routes approvals.

03

Trigger orchestration

Approved status starts the Workflow Builder process.

04

Read the linked record

Dynamics 365 retrieves parent/guardian contact and school fields.

05

Generate the agreement

Workflow data populates a Web Form or eSignature template.

06

Collect consent

Parent reviews and signs, including address-change section.

07

Update and archive

Verified fields write to Dynamics 365; PDF archives to SharePoint.

08

Ask and act

Copilot queries status and initiates configured actions via MCP.

Next steps

Start with one process, expand from there.

Begin with one agreement type, one Dynamics record type, one SharePoint archive path, and a small set of read-only Copilot questions. Add write actions only after permissions, auditability, and human-review controls are verified.

Working reference architecture. Revalidate product boundaries against the customer's tenant, region, licensing, and current release status before external commitment.
