# Prompts by role

A prompt is a question you type into your AI tool, such as Claude or ChatGPT. Copy it, replace the \[brackets\], and run it. Read-only prompts answer straight away. Anything that changes a document waits for your approval, and missing information is flagged rather than guessed.

Run the [connector test](/PSA/AI-Cookbook-v2/connect/#test-connector) first.

[Account executive](/PSA/AI-Cookbook-v2/prompts/account-executive/) — 12 prompts · Know where a customer's agreement stands before the next call, and get order forms out without rework.

[Sales leader and RevOps](/PSA/AI-Cookbook-v2/prompts/sales-leader-and-revops/) — 8 prompts · See which deals are signed, stalled or waiting on the customer, without building a report by hand.

[Procurement manager](/PSA/AI-Cookbook-v2/prompts/procurement-manager/) — 12 prompts · Know which vendor NDAs and contracts exist, when they renew, and how the terms compare.

[Legal operations](/PSA/AI-Cookbook-v2/prompts/legal-operations/) — 12 prompts · Find the terms, deadlines and non-standard clauses that matter, quickly and with the source cited.

[Finance and revenue ops](/PSA/AI-Cookbook-v2/prompts/finance-and-revenue-ops/) — 10 prompts · Confirm revenue documents are executed before close and flag unusual payment terms.

[CX and FINS operations](/PSA/AI-Cookbook-v2/prompts/cx-and-fins-operations/) — 9 prompts · Move account updates and account openings through without missing fields or stalled steps.
