Troubleshooting

SymptomLikely causeFix
No agreements visibleConnected to production, or Agreement Manager still extractingReconnect with sandbox credentials. Wait 3–5 minutes per document. Ask: What Docusign account am I connected to?
Agreement fields blank or partialExtraction still running, or unsupported fileClick Re-analyze and wait five minutes.
Workflow not listedWorkflow is in DraftOpen the workflow under Workflows and Publish. Wait about 60 seconds.
Agent cannot find templatesNo templates in the sandboxImport the template from the sample agreement pack under Document Templates.
Envelope send failsRequired fields empty, or sandbox email restrictionTry a different recipient. Compare with a manual send using the same values.
Old data returnedAgent reuses earlier chat resultsAsk it to refresh agreements from Docusign.
Workflow says started but did not runTrigger inputs did not match required fieldsCheck the trigger inputs in the activity map, or run the workflow manually in Maestro.
No tools/call in the activity mapGenerative orchestration off, or instructions allow answering from memoryTurn on generative orchestration. Require an MCP call for any live Docusign state. Republish.
Agent missing from Teams or M365 CopilotChannel off, not published, or tenant approval pendingConfirm the channel is on and published. Ask your tenant admin to approve. Allow propagation time.
